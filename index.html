How to Play

Tap a bottle to pick up the top color segment, then tap another to pour it. You may only pour onto an empty bottle or onto the same color, and a bottle holds at most 4 units. Sort every color into its own bottle to win!

⟲ Shuffle rearranges the bottles and ⚗ Extra Tube adds an empty bottle. Earn boosters from daily chests and winning boss levels!
